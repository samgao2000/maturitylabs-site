Three ways we work with you

Start with any of them. Most clients begin with an assessment, then decide.

  • AI maturity appraisal

    An independent, evidence-based appraisal against CMMI AI Maturity (AIM). You get a rating your board, customers and investors can rely on, plus a clear list of gaps.

  • AI transformation coaching

    A structured program built on our IMPACT method: Intent, Measure, Plan, Act, Check, Transformation. We work alongside your teams until AI is part of how work gets done, with results you can measure.

  • AI agents for your operations

    We design and build AI agents for real workflows, such as sales pipelines, delivery and back office, and hand them over in production.

How an engagement runs

  1. Readiness call

    Thirty minutes, free. We learn your goals and tell you honestly whether we can help.

  2. Gap analysis

    We review how AI is used today and compare it with the maturity level you are aiming for.

  3. Roadmap

    A prioritized path from where you are to where you want to be, with owners and milestones.

  4. Coach, build or appraise

    We help you execute, then confirm progress with a formal appraisal when you are ready.

Coaching and appraisal for the same client are always delivered by different appraisers, so every rating stays independent.
